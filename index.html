OFFICIAL GROUP GUIDELINES

Keep the community focused

• Request only one paper/resource at a time with complete details.

• Keep the official WhatsApp group for resource access and student support.

• Avoid unnecessary, repeated or unrelated messages.

• Do not disturb other students or interfere with automated/bot resource operations.

• Some resources may not be available when the relevant official authority has not released them.

• Activity that disrupts students or automated operations may result in removal from the group.
